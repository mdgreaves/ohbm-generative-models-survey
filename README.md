# What Is a Generative Model?  

This repository contains the de-identified survey data and analysis code supporting the paper:

> **Greaves, M. D., Novelli, L., Breakspear, M., & Razi, A.**  
> *What Is a Generative Model? Definitions, Disagreements, and Evaluation in Human Neuroimaging*  
> bioRxiv preprint (link to be added)
> [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/1133883596.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18238047)


## Overview

The project reports results from a survey of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM) community examining how *generative models* are defined, used, and evaluated in practice. The analysis focuses on:

- Conceptual definitions of generative models
- Areas of agreement and disagreement across model classes
- Criteria used to evaluate model quality
- Demographic and disciplinary context of respondents

To protect participant confidentiality, only **revised codes and thematic variables** are shared. No free-text responses, fine-grained initial codes, or direct identifiers are included.

## Repository contents

- `data/OHBM_survey_results_public.csv`  
  De-identified survey dataset containing revised codes, thematic labels, and demographic variables.

- `analysis_and_figure.m`  
  MATLAB script that reproduces all quantitative analyses and Figure 1 reported in the paper. No additional toolboxes beyond base MATLAB are required.

## Notes on data sharing

- The original free-text survey responses are **not shared** due to identifiability risks.
- Initial fine-grained codes were consolidated into revised codes and themes prior to public release.
- All shared data are de-identified.

Full details of the survey design, questions, recruitment, and qualitative analysis procedures are provided in the Supporting Information and the bioRxiv preprint.
